ARTISTRY

About The Project

Joseph Beuys famously said "Everyone Can Be An Artist". Artsy aims to let everyone monetise their own pieces of art. Wherever you are just making first steps into the world of art or an art student you can present to the wider audience. The purpose of the application is to help art galleries promote an independent artists and let audience auction their pieces of art.

The Goal

Purpose of the app is to help art galleries to promote an independent artists and build the community

My Role & Responsibilities

The project involved UI and UX Design process in order to build the innovative solution. My involvement included:

UX Design

After understanding of the background research user flows, personas, wireframes and protypes has been created.

Research

The interviews has been conducted with selected group of users. After design modification they has been repeated.

Prototype Testing

Three rounds of usability testing has been conducted on selected user flows of the app. Then the protype has been updated according to insights.

Approach

I have used design thinking methodology, applying alongside the user-centered design process to make sure that the overall design decisions are aligned with user needs.

Research

Research Outcomes

Since art community values both digital and in-person experiences I had to learn more about art market and the users. I conducted a desk research to study the market trends, needs and competitors. The goal of the research was to gather crucial insights about the market as well as to understand the perspective of the end users.

Art Market Trends Insights

source: sothebys

Competitive Analysis

NY ArtBeat

  1. More like a map of art in NY
  2. Lack of themes
  3. Too many bold adds

Artsy

  1. Excellent selections of contemporary and ancient artists
  2. Interesting 'View in a room' feature
  3. Mix of famous and independent artists
  4. categorized by events or place type rather than art period or style
  5. great selection
  6. confusing descriptions making it hard to evaluate the purchase
I have conducted a usability study which has been involving surveys and interviews that helped me create an empathy maps and better understand user journey and user expectations. I found out that users are equally interested in the art as much as in artist profile. They would prefer to auction pieces of art rather than pay the fixed price. They find it engaging.

User Research:Pain Points

Extenstion: Users would love to be able to explore artists detailed profile to learn about artists.
Interaction: Having a wishlist and opportunity to connect and contact an artist.
